Policy settings

Business rules the team changes without a developer

Late payment

Daily interest rate
Simple daily interest on outstanding principal. Never compounds — accrued interest is not itself charged interest.
Grace period
Days after the due date before interest starts. With a 10 Aug due date and 3 days grace, the first charge is dated 14 Aug.
Interest cap
Ceiling on total interest as a share of principal. 0 % means uncapped.
Flat late fee
One-off administrative charge applied when an invoice first goes overdue.
